All colour textures share one palette of the colours you choose. Bones, expressions, spring bones, materials and the license (meta) stay as they are, and the VRM version stays the same (0.x or 1.0), so the file works in other VRM apps too. Textures are set to sharp pixels (nearest filter).
The preview on the left is drawn by Pyxel, which adds its own colour limits; other apps show the textures exactly.

Picture → voxel VRM

Use only pictures you made or may use (for AI pictures: check the terms of the service you used). No real people without their consent, and no characters that belong to someone else.
Open a picture of one character, standing, seen from the front, on a plain background (or a transparent PNG). Arms a little away from the body work best.
A turnaround sheet (front, side, back side by side, in a T-pose) gives a real depth and back: the views are found automatically. A bright green background (#00FF00) cuts out best.

About

Your file never leaves your device: it is read and converted inside this page.

License of the VRM you open

The retro VRM is a modified version of your avatar. This tool refuses avatars whose license forbids modification. For the others, follow the original license when you use or share the result (for example, an avatar that may not be redistributed stays for your own use).
